3. Class Timetable: CLASS 10 Rose

Class Teacher: Komal
1

Equal Instructional Hours

At 40 mins × 6 periods = 240 mins (4 hours) of teaching per day. At 60 mins × 4 periods = 240 mins (4 hours)! Teachers and students get the exact same total teaching time, but in deeper, uninterrupted lecture blocks.

2

Shared Faculty Handling

Out of 51 teachers, only 5 teach across Senior and other wings (e.g. Komal, Mraganshi). The timetable engine schedules shared teachers during aligned break windows or dedicates them to senior periods.

3

Dispersal Stays 01:10 PM

Both schedules end cleanly at 01:10 PM! School bus routes, parent pickups, and dispersal bells for Classes 9 to 12 remain 100% synchronized with the rest of the school.
